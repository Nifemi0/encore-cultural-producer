# Encore visual direction

## Product perception

Encore should feel intelligent, imaginative, assured, and ready for serious creative work.

## Selected direction

**Cultural signals in orbit** uses luminous connected objects to make Encore's mechanism visible: different cultural domains become valuable when the agent finds a credible relationship between them.

The direction draws principles from [Lead Bank](https://www.lead.bank/): an atmospheric pale canvas, large direct typography, concentrated blue-violet color, generous spacing, and one sculptural focal visual. Encore uses an original composition, copy, mark, palette, and product layout.

## Visual system

- **Canvas:** icy white and pale blue, with dark ink for primary text.
- **Accent:** cobalt moving into violet; coral appears only inside the visual as a warm signal.
- **Type:** Manrope for large, plain, tightly spaced headlines; DM Mono for compact evidence and system labels.
- **Geometry:** rounded action pills and large soft panels; fine borders organize dense product content.
- **Motion:** the hero pins during the opening scroll. The 3D constellation enters from depth with blur and rotation, resolves into sharp focus, then scales past the viewer while the copy recedes. Short hover lifts support later content. Reduced-motion preferences disable the scene.
- **Navigation:** transparent full-width header at the top; after the user scrolls it condenses into a floating white capsule with blur and shadow.
- **Image:** an original generated 3D constellation of venue, sound, fashion, film, and city-inspired forms connected by light paths.

## Component translation

- The hero pairs the product promise with the cultural constellation.
- A four-part signal strip explains the workflow in one scan.
- The intelligence section visualizes anchor, place, brand, and artist discovery as a connected system.
- Three stacked route cards establish the creative range before the visitor reaches the tool.
- A dossier preview demonstrates how evidence and operational verification travel with the idea.
- The brief sits in one elevated white studio panel.
- Research steps become individual signal tiles.
- Concepts use related white, periwinkle, and warm-white surfaces.
- The final dossier moves onto a deep ink-blue presentation surface.
